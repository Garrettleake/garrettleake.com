What Team Ares was

Team Ares started in 2020 as a Fortnite team and became a worldwide marketing, apparel and entertainment organization headquartered in Scottsdale, Arizona. The business had three pillars: marketing and entertainment (video production, social advertising, and sponsored placements across our creators), apparel (scheduled merch drops and community events), and the creator network itself — content creators on YouTube and TikTok plus a roster of Twitch streamers, packaged into one audience a brand could buy.

My own esports team was bought out by Team Ares when I was 16, and I came in as an owner and CEO. I co-owned the company with three partners who ran content, operations and creator support. My lane was the business: I built the sponsor deck, priced and sold the packages, managed the partners, planned the events, and directed the brand and every piece of creative that went out.

Sponsorships and brand partners

Sponsorship was the revenue engine and it was mine end to end: outreach, the deck, pricing, contracts, and delivery. Recent brand partners on the pitch sheet were SCUF Gaming, Epic Games, Samsung and AXE; other deals I closed and delivered include the NXT LVL Gamer Shots recruitment campaign, the ExitLag partnership (October 2023), the Ares × Rematch.GG $2,500 NAE Duos Cash Cup (January 2022), and an Arrivant activation.

Packages ran from $4,999 (five custom creator ads, shoutouts, two live-stream activations) to a $74,999 awareness campaign (100 creator ads, 100 shoutouts, month-long logo and bio placements). Under the packages sat a rate card I kept for 15+ creators — $125 to $7,000 per ad, $150 to $200 per stream hour — so every deal could be priced from real numbers.

Merch: four collections

I ran apparel from concept to sell-through — product design, mockups, the store, the drop video, and the marketing around each release. The "God of War" drop (June 2021) shipped Red, Gold and War editions. "Art of War" (October 2021) was a tee-and-hoodie release to mark 50,000 subscribers and sold out. The "Claim the Throne" collection (2022) was the biggest: Crown and Sword lines, hats and jerseys, with a full video advertisement produced in-house. The 2023 drop went with the rebrand: a vintage-washed black tee carrying the new ARES wordmark on the chest, three marks down the sleeve and a tone-on-tone mark across the back, alongside the purple "Ares Uprise" sweatshirt and sticker designs.

Questions people ask about Team Ares

What is Team Ares?

Team Ares (Team Ares LLC) was an esports and creator organization headquartered in Scottsdale, Arizona, active from 2020 to 2023. It started as a Fortnite team and grew into a marketing, apparel and entertainment brand built around a network of YouTube, TikTok and Twitch creators.

Who owned Team Ares?

Garrett Leake was an owner and the CEO of Team Ares LLC, serving as Head of Business and Sales & Event Manager. He co-owned the company with three partners who ran content, operations and creator support.

How big was Team Ares?

At its peak the Team Ares network reached 277 million views in a single 30-day period and over one billion views combined, across roughly 17.6 million followers. The main Ares YouTube channel passed 100,000 subscribers, the TikTok account reached 270,000 followers, and Instagram reached 134,000.

Who sponsored Team Ares?

Brand partners included SCUF Gaming, Epic Games, Samsung, AXE, ExitLag, NXT LVL Gamer Shots, Rematch.GG and Arrivant. Sponsorship packages ranged from $4,999 to $74,999 and were sold and managed by Garrett Leake.

What games did Team Ares play?

Team Ares was rooted in Fortnite. Its creators produced Fortnite montages, tutorials, tournaments and recruitment challenges, and the org hosted cash-prize Fortnite events such as the Ares × Rematch.GG $2,500 NAE Duos Cash Cup.

Is Team Ares still active?

Team Ares has been inactive since 2023. Its last major public update was the October 2023 rebrand. The YouTube and X channels remain online, and Garrett Leake no longer runs the organization day-to-day.
