What it is

GKHT Exotics is an exotic and luxury car rental brand. Rather than owning a lot, I broker a fleet of more than 45 vehicles from partner suppliers — Ferrari 296 GTB, Rolls-Royce Cullinan, Lamborghini Urus, BMW M4, Porsche, Mercedes, Tesla — and deliver the car to the client instead of making them come to a dealership. I also rent out one of my own cars alongside the fleet.

What I built

The brand, the positioning ("Drive Without Compromise"), the booking operation, and the website. The site is a 65-page static build on GitHub Pages with a page for every vehicle and every market, clean URLs, structured data for every product, and social previews — built so that someone searching for a specific car in a specific city finds the right page. It's the same operator's approach I took at Team Ares: own the numbers, own the creative, own the sale.

Questions about GKHT Exotics

What is GKHT Exotics?

GKHT Exotics is an exotic and luxury car rental company owned and operated by Garrett Leake. It brokers a fleet of 45+ vehicles in Los Angeles and Phoenix / Scottsdale and delivers cars directly to the client.

Who owns GKHT Exotics?

Garrett Leake founded and owns GKHT Exotics. He built the brand, the website and the booking operation, and rents out one of his own cars alongside the brokered fleet.
