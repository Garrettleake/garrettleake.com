About

Built from the community up

I didn't come into brand work from an agency. I came in from the other side of the screen — as the streamer, then the manager, then the owner signing the sponsor.

I started streaming on Twitch at 13. By 16 I'd moved into management and founded my own esports team, which was later acquired by Team Ares — where I became an owner and CEO.

At Ares I owned the business side end to end: sponsorship sales and management, the sponsor deck, every advertisement and merch drop, and the creative direction behind the brand. The org grew to a channel of over 100K subscribers, a Shorts channel over 60K, 30K+ on X and 100K+ on Instagram, with a creator network that peaked at 277 million views in 30 days — over one billion views combined.

Today I work as a brand and creative director — bringing that same operator's view to partnerships, identity, and content that actually moves numbers.
